# v2.9.8 "Cadence" — performance, the MiSTer menu, and libnx

The fifth release of the line to v3.0.0
([line plan](v2.9.4-to-v3.0.0-line-plan.md), v2.9.8 row). v2.9.7 closed
platform gaps. This release measures the performance leads that were never
measured with a working tool, adds the MiSTer menu options that co-simulation
can partly check, and settles the libretro `platform=libnx` question.

## Maintainer decisions (2026-09-30)

| Question | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Codename | **Cadence** |
| S2, the slot-scheduled SDRAM arbiter | **Stays after v3.0.0**, as decided at v2.9.1. Since v2.9.1 put CHR in its own bank, nothing is over budget. |
| MiSTer menu options | **All three**: aspect ratio and integer scaling through `video_freak`, overscan crop, and a palette choice. Labelled "compiled, timing-closed, not seen on hardware" |
| `platform=libnx` | **Dropped with an explicit error.** It is not deleted, because a deleted branch would fall through to a host build |

## What the survey found before any code

- **The line plan's SDRAM figures are stale.** "24/24" (the CPU's PRG deadline) and
  "23 against a budget of 22" (a CHR fetch) are v2.6.16 console measurements.
  v2.9.1 moved CHR to its own SDRAM bank (S1, NR-08), and the re-measured
  worst cases are **CHR 18 of 22 and PRG 22 of 24** on all three workloads
  (sibling `docs/sdram.md`). The CHR figure was never a defect: its gate
  threshold is 31. The line plan, the sibling's `submission-case.md` and the
  arbiter header still quote the old numbers, and are corrected here.
- **Two plans disagreed about S2.** The line plan put it at v2.9.8.
  `v2.9.x-final-audit-and-hardware-plan.md` recorded "decided at v2.9.1: after
  v3.0.0". The maintainer kept the v2.9.1 decision.
- **`video_freak` is in `sys/` but not instantiated.** `emu.sv` drives
  `VIDEO_ARX/ARY` directly and omits `[ARC1]`/`[ARC2]` for that reason. There
  is no crop and no palette option; `rtl/palette.sv` is the fixed FBX "Smooth"
  table.
- **The second palette must not come from `palette_gen.rs`.** That file's
  `// Provenance:` header discloses a derived region (Bisqwit's method, ares's
  colour integration), which ADR 0037 makes a black box for HDL.
  The **2C03 RGB palette** from NESdev "PPU palettes" is used instead. It is
  published hardware DAC data (`255 * DAC / 7`), and the oracle already renders
  it for the Vs. System. Its RGB emphasis rule differs from the 2C02's: an
  emphasis bit forces its own channel to full scale.
- **Seven perf rejections were measured with the broken tool.** Before v2.9.1,
  `ab_check.sh` built both sides into one target directory. G3, G4, G5, G6, G9,
  D1 and D3 are therefore unverified (`docs/performance.md` v2.9.1 section).
  D2 and D4 were never measured. G2 and the palette-mirror and unmapped-read
  leads have v2.9.1 ceilings and rejected candidates.
- **libnx cannot build.** `aarch64-nintendo-switch-freestanding` is tier 3 with
  no `std`; the core needs `std::fs` and `catch_unwind`. No libretro buildbot
  Switch template exists for Rust. The v2.9.0 re-audit left it as SCOPE.

## Rules for this release

- Perf candidates must be byte-identical. AccuracyCoin 144/144 and nestest
  0-diff are re-run on anything adopted.
- **Serialise heavy jobs.** An A/B run needs a quiet host (one-minute load under
  1.5), and a Quartus seed sweep saturates it. They never run together.
- **No hardware claim.** The menu options are compiled and timing-closed, not
  seen on hardware.
- **Provenance:** the MiSTer template's `video_freak` is framework code, not an
  NES core. The 2C03 table comes from NESdev.

## Out of scope

- S2 and every later SDRAM feature (after v3.0.0).
- The sibling's oracle-pin move (v2.9.9).
- Hardware verification (v3.x).
