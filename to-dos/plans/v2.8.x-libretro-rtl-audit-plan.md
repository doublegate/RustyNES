# v2.8.x — the libretro and RTL audits, and the off-die build

The second of three minor lines to the SuperStation One core at **v3.0.0**
([ADR 0041](../../docs/adr/0041-hardware-release-is-v3.0.0.md)). It acts on the
**libretro** and **RTL** audits in [`docs/audits/`](../../docs/audits/README.md),
and it is the line that changes the bitstream.

## The RTL report needs more suspicion than the others

Its calibration refuted two of its defects outright (the DC-blocker overflow and
the pulse-1 sweep mute), refuted half of a third (DQM at CAS latency 2), and found
its resource table does not match the Quartus fit report. What held up was real
and worth fixing: CKE raised with the first PRECHARGE, the arbiter's byte-lane
select, the unsynchronised reset, the MMC1 filter ordering, and the SDRAM pins
with no timing constraints at all. The
[RTL ledger](../../docs/audits/rtl-disposition.md) records every verdict.

So in this line, **every RTL claim is re-derived and every number is measured**,
and every fix:

- is written from nesdev, the datasheets and JEDEC, under the
  [ADR 0037](../../docs/adr/0037-mister-fpga-core-independent-hdl-implementation.md)
  firewall. Before reading any oracle source to form an RTL hypothesis, run
  `grep -rn "^// Provenance:" crates/*/src/*.rs` and stay out of the listed regions;
- lands with a co-simulation gate that **fails first** (a new golden, or
  `tb/mutate.sh` evidence);
- is graded by `tb/regress.sh`'s `TOTAL:` line, run from a frozen copy.

## Open questions carried into this line

- **Which SDRAM part the SuperStation One actually has.** The repository documents
  the AS4C32M16SB. `rtl/sdram.sv:41-46` describes 128 MB as two 64 MB chips with an
  inverted second chip select, while `docs/HARDWARE_TESTING.md` calls the SS1's
  128 MB a single module. The v2.8.4 constraints are written for the documented
  part and **marked provisional**; the part number is read off the board at
  v2.9.2's Strand A.
- **Whether the SS1 loads a DE10-Nano bitstream.** Two spec sources name its FPGA
  as a Cyclone V SX 5CSXFC6D6F31 while this build targets 5CSEBA6U23I7; stock
  MiSTer cores are reported running on it unmodified. Also settled at v2.9.2,
  Strand A, on the stock core. Nothing in this line depends on the answer, and
  everything in v2.9.x does.

## Out of scope for this line

The core and frontend audits (v2.7.x); the re-audit, the final seed sweep and the
board (v2.9.x); PAL, which is not a supported build and is deferred past v3.0.0.
