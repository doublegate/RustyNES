## Risks

- **Fitter pressure:**
  - v3.6.2's release RTL closed both builds at one seed in eight, and FDS
    adds RTL and SDRAM traffic.
  - The fitter study runs before the release sweep, with the M10K move and a
    9-16 sweep (as at v3.6.0) as fallbacks.
- **Load-slot reuse:** a reused CONF_STR index breaks ROM or palette loading
  silently, so it gets its own gate.
- **The pull toward `fds.rs`:** it is derived code. A disagreement is settled
  by black-box comparison, not by reading it; rung 4 needs an ADR 0037
  amendment first.
- **Swift compiles only in CI.**
- **The live Durable Object** is a cost and abuse surface. Rate limits and a
  kill switch are in place before it is deployed, as for v3.4.1.
- **Forensics and spectating change the netplay protocol.** Older peers are
  refused with a reason, and the release notes say so.
