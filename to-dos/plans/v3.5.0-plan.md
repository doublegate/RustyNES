## Decisions and deferrals (2026-10-20/21)

- **The Lite tier (maintainer).** `RustyNES_Lite` keeps the boards it carried in
  v3.4.x; every board this release adds (Bandai FCG 16/153/159, VRC7 85, MMC5,
  VRC6, Namco 163) is off-die only. The Lite build refuses those boards as an
  unsupported mapper (`offdie_only()` in the sibling's `rtl/ines_header.sv`),
  and the sibling's `tier-gate` asks the RTL's own decision about every mapper
  and submapper at both builds' parameters and compares the result with the
  "Which build carries which board" table in `docs/rung7-mappers.md`.
- **VRC7's OPLL moves to v3.8.0 (maintainer, D15).** Mapper 85 ships with its
  banking, WRAM and IRQ, off-die only, and silent. The documentation does not
  determine the modulation depth, the envelope steps, the table resolution and
  DAC curve, the tremolo depth, the LFO shapes, the key-on reset or the
  percussive release. Ticket `T-MISTER-VRC7-OPLL`, v3.8.0 row 9.
- **The Miracle Piano is not modelled.** The oracle has no model to gate it
  against, and its NESdev page describes the protocol only as "appears to be
  via clocked shift registers", so nothing could gate an implementation. Row 6 ships the keyboard, the
  P2 microphone and the Power Pad.
- **The Power Pad's serial layout is recorded as derived from Mesen2
  (maintainer).** The oracle's `input_device.rs` cited "Mesen's
  implementation" with no Provenance header; it now carries one (`4c1fde15`),
  and the sibling's `docs/provenance.md` discloses the read of that file.
- **Save-state compatibility breaks on the MiSTer core.** The new boards and
  the peripherals change the layout fingerprint, so v3.4.x states are refused
  with a reason, and the OSD's Controllers option moved to status bits 23:21
  (an older config reads as Gamepads). Both go in the release notes.

## Risks

- **The on-die build is nearly full, and this release's boards are the large ones.**
  At v3.4.0 `RustyNES_Lite` uses 36,937 of 41,910 ALMs (88%) and 468 of 553 M10K
  (85%), up from 63% of ALMs at v3.3.0. Two of eight seeds miss setup, and the
  off-die build needed a multicycle exception on its PRG crossing. Rows 4-6 add
  MMC5, N163, VRC6, VRC7 with an OPLL, and Bandai FCG. **Decision for the maintainer
  at the start of v3.5.0:** whether Lite becomes a mapper tier (the discrete boards
  and MMC1-4 class, with each build's mapper list in the honesty gate) and the large
  boards and expansion audio ship off-die only. Research of 2026-10-18 (public docs,
  issue and forum text only; no NES core's source; the write-up is in the oracle's
  gitignored `salvaged/evidence/v3.4.0-research/`) found that mainline NES_MiSTer
  requires the SDRAM add-on, keeps everything in one build, has no on-die variant,
  ships VRC7 without save states, and publishes no utilisation figure. Its
  suggestions, labelled as inference: time-multiplex the OPLL and N163 channels with
  their state in block RAM (the chips process channels serially), share one
  expansion-audio mixer slot, save block-RAM-held state through the RAM's own port
  rather than the scan chain, and judge timing by the pass fraction of a sweep.
- VRC7's OPLL is the largest audio block and is Provenance-headered in the
  oracle (`opll.rs`). If documentation does not suffice, the escalation is an
  ADR 0037 amendment naming the maintainer (D15), never a silent read. The
  board can move to v3.8.0 rather than the release waiting on it.
