# v3.7.1: the spectator id, and faster gates (PROPOSED)

A patch release after v3.7.0 "Spindle" (2026-11-10), proposed on 2026-11-12.
**Whether to cut it is the maintainer's call**: nothing here is started
because of this plan. If it is not cut, every row moves to
[`v3.8.0-plan.md`](v3.8.0-plan.md), section "Carry-overs", with the same
gate. The codename is asked at the cut.

## Why a patch release (the recommendation)

**Recommended: yes, a small v3.7.1 before v3.8.0.** The reasons:

- **The shipped desktop binaries leak spectator access.** v3.7.0 minted one
  random spectator match id, saved it, and re-hosted every later match under
  it. Viewers present no key, so anyone once given the id could watch every
  match the host played afterwards. The relay that serves them has been live
  since 2026-11-11 (`.github/release-notes/v3.7.0.md`). The fix, a fresh id
  per match, is on `main` (#648, `1b605a22`, ADR 0046 addendum) and reaches
  users only through a release. v3.8.0 starts with a fitter study and two
  derived-region audio rows, so it is weeks away.
- **What is already on `main` is ready.** The dependency sweep (#649,
  `161c457d`) passed the full suite with the commercial corpus, with no
  golden, snapshot or frame moved (CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]`). Both changes
  are user-visible and already have CHANGELOG entries.
- **Nothing emulated changes.** The epoch stays 10, the netplay protocol
  stays 10 (`"RN10"`) and save states, movies and sessions carry over. The
  MiSTer core changes no RTL, so no seed sweep is needed.
- **The rest is cheap, and pays off at v3.8.0.** The ladder pool cuts a
  ladder from about ten hours serial to under three (sibling `ee28245`). The
  parallel coverage boot targets 108 minutes of the full suite (#648). Both
  are gates v3.8.0 runs many times; adopting them inside v3.8.0 would mean
  re-proving each gate in the middle of a release.
- **Precedent.** v3.4.2, v3.5.1, v3.6.1 and v3.6.2 were patch releases of
  this size.

**Against:**

- Every release costs a release-PR ceremony and Copilot review credits in
  both repositories (the 2026-11-12 cost audit, `docs/agents/ci-and-release.md`
  on #650).
- The MiSTer core's own v3.7.0 (sibling #74) is still open. Its bitstream
  sweep on the review-fix RTL had closed one compile of the twelve finished
  on 2026-11-12 (`T-MISTER-FITTER-PRESSURE`).
  v3.7.1 cannot finish before it, so the sibling side of v3.7.1 starts only
  once #74 is merged.

## Risks

- **A pool can hide an ordering dependency.** Two gates that share a scratch
  file or a ROM pass alone and fail together, or the reverse. The planted
  FAIL and the same-commit comparison are what catch it. A previous-commit
  comparison (`ee28245`) cannot, which is why row 10 runs again.
- **Timing measured on a loaded host is not a measurement.** The 7-ROM
  coverage smoke (17.0 s on 4 workers) and the audit's load average of 17-20
  on 20 cores both say so. Rows 4 and 7 wait for a quiet host, and row 7
  needs its second pair (`docs/agents/measurement-discipline.md`).
- **The old spectator id.** A host who typed a 32-hex-digit id has it
  cleared too, with a note on stderr (#648). The release notes say so.
- **Sequencing.** The sibling half waits on #74, and #74 waits on a seed
  sweep. If that takes days, the oracle half can ship alone with the v3.7.0
  bitstreams re-attached. Which to do is for the maintainer.

## Out of scope

- **Any emulation, format or protocol change**: epoch 10, protocol 10 and
  the v3.7.0 `.rnm` and `.rns` formats carry over.
- **RTL changes**, and so a seed sweep: the fitter study is v3.8.0's entry
  item (E1).
- **`T-FDS-WIKI-DIVERGENCE`**, including the two departures recorded on
  2026-11-12: v3.8.0, row 5a.
- **The ladder's build-flag measurements** (`-O3 -march=native`, clang, PGO,
  Verilator `--threads`): part of `T-MISTER-LADDER-PARALLEL`, but none
  changes a verdict, so they wait for v3.8.0.
- **Maintainer settings** (Copilot review effort, the sibling's Dependabot
  runner): listed in `v3.8.0-plan.md`, section "Maintainer settings"; not
  release items.
