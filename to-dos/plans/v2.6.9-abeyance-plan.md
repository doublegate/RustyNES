# v2.6.9 "Abeyance" — an exclusion hides improvement as well as regression

Abeyance is a state of temporary suspension in which a right still exists but
nobody is exercising it. That is what a deny-list entry is: the coverage is
still there, still computable, and nothing looks at it.

v2.6.8 established that a deny list is an assertion about the thing under test
and re-measured six entries, retiring four. This release goes one level down and
finds that the mechanism itself is wrong-shaped: **a denied stream is denied
ENTIRELY**, so a golden whose divergence is one checkpoint out of 88 forfeits
the other 87 — and, worse, an entry that silently *improves* is as invisible as
one that silently regresses.

## Measured before planning

Both remaining denied streams were re-measured, per-cycle, on all nine fields:

| golden | v2.6.2 declaration | measured at v2.6.9 |
|---|---|---|
| `apuconflict039` | "9 bus divergences" — 3 the conflict proper, 6 bit-6 frame-IRQ timing | **3**, cycles 32862-32864, `bus_data` `$40` vs `$10` |
| `ppuoamcorrupt052` | "a real divergence, needs its own diagnosis" (v2.6.8) | **1**, cycle 70627, `bus_data` `$80` vs `$00` |

**Six of `apuconflict039`'s nine divergences closed and nobody noticed.** They
were the bit-6 pair — a `$4015` read plus the store of what it returned — and
v2.6.7's `$4017` interrupt-clear split is the obvious candidate. Invisible
because the whole stream is denied, in both directions.

**`ppuoamcorrupt052` did not need a diagnosis; it had one.** v2.6.8's prose said
it "needs its own diagnosis" when ledger 3.19 already records it in full: the
DUT implements all three OAM-corruption seed windows from AccuracyCoin's own
source, the oracle implements one (a TriCNES port), **the DUT implements more of
the documented rule than the oracle does, and no gate can adjudicate**. The one
differing cycle is `LDA $2004` after `OAMADDR <- $80`: row 16 reads back `$80`
uncorrupted on the oracle and `$00` corrupted on the DUT, which is precisely the
asymmetry 3.19 describes. That prose is corrected here rather than restated.

## The deliverable

1. **`tb/obs_diff9.py`** — the missing instrument. `bus_diff.py` compares FOUR
   fields per cycle; `ckpt_diff.py` hashes nine but only every 4096 cycles, and
   its own failure message ends "re-run that window with `--bus-out` on both
   sides to find the cycle", which until now meant doing it by hand. This names
   the first differing cycle, the field, both values, and the SHAPE across the
   whole stream — which found both numbers above in one command each.

2. **A scoped allowance replacing the binary deny.** As planned this was
   `ckpt_diff.py --expect-diff`, a list of checkpoint indices known to differ.
   **That design was implemented, measured, and REFUTED** — recorded here rather
   than quietly replaced, because the refutation is the reusable part.

   `checkpoint.h` chains a **rolling** FNV-1a. One divergent cycle therefore
   poisons every checkpoint after it, so allowing `ppuoamcorrupt052`'s first
   differing index moved the failure straight to the next one; allowing the rest
   is the all-or-nothing deny it was meant to replace. An allowance by index
   cannot work on a rolling hash, and "recovering 87 of 88" was never available
   by that route. The mutation pass is what found this, before it shipped.

   What replaces it is `obs_diff9.py --allow-cycle`, which puts the allowance on
   the **per-cycle** comparison, where there is no such coupling. Both failure
   clauses survive intact:
   - any cycle differing that is **not** allowed — a regression; and
   - any allowed cycle that now **matches** — a stale allowance.

   The second clause is still the point, and it is what would have caught six
   divergences closing unnoticed.

3. **Both streams re-gated**, and the recovered coverage is larger than planned
   rather than smaller: an attributed difference now costs **one cycle**, not
   seventy-one checkpoints.

   | golden | before | after |
   |---|---|---|
   | `apuconflict039` | 0 compared | **all 357,361 cycles and all 88 checkpoints** — no allowance needed |
   | `ppuoamcorrupt052` | 0 compared | **357,360 of 357,361 cycles**, one allowed and attributed |

## Rung 7, which the plan did not scope and the maintainer asked for

The cartridge was not in this release's original deliverable; it was requested
mid-cycle and is the larger half of what shipped. Its full record, including the
two PPU defects the MMC3 counter found on the first day anything consumed A12,
is `RustyNES_MiSTer/docs/rung7-mappers.md`.

**One finding belongs here rather than there, because it is about this
programme's own habits.** `mapper4mmc3irq065` is deliberately unregistered, and
the comment justifying that quoted 180 IRQ assertions against 158 and a first
divergence at cycle 60,104 — **both figures invalidated the same day, by the two
PPU fixes in the same change**. Re-measured at the release commit: 120/120
assertions, 158/158 interrupts taken, 950 of 178,676 cycles diverging from
62,166. An exclusion whose stated reason has expired is precisely the defect
v2.6.8 spent an entire release on, recurring one release later inside this
release's own new code — which is the argument for re-measuring an exclusion at
the commit that ships it, not at the commit that wrote it.

## The fix, mutated

The remediation rule is to mutate the thing that was WRONG, not its
neighbourhood. Reverting `cpu_main.cpp`'s held-cycle `bus_data` to the stale
local -- the exact defect -- is **CAUGHT by both** gates that fixing it opened:

| gate | verdict |
|---|---|
| `obs9-gate` | **CAUGHT** — 3 cycles differ, first at 32,862 |
| `ckpt-gate` | **CAUGHT** — checkpoint hash mismatch |

Those three cycles are the ones still differing when v2.6.9 measured the stream,
and the mutation restores exactly them, which is what makes it the right
mutation rather than a nearby one. Restored from a pre-edit snapshot, never from
`git checkout`.

## Risks

1. **The allowance becomes a place to hide a real defect.** Mitigated by clause
   3 — an entry that stops being needed fails the gate — and by requiring each
   index to carry its cycle range and attribution at the call site.
2. **`apuconflict039`'s remaining 3 may be a DUT defect rather than a declared
   difference.** **RESOLVED, and it was neither.** It was the HARNESS: on a
   cycle the CPU is held, `tb/cpu_main.cpp` built the `Observable`'s `bus_data`
   from a stale local rather than from the RTL's own latch. Taking it from the
   latch makes the stream identical on every field of every cycle. The local is
   dead and deleted, and the allowance shrinks to one stream exactly as this
   risk anticipated — for a reason it did not.

   The direction is the finding. The evidence pointed at the DUT, the reasoning
   from AccuracyCoin's rule was sound, and the defect was in the instrument
   reading the DUT. A "declared diagnostic" is a claim about the thing under
   test; this one was a claim about the measurement, and the phrase "by design"
   is what kept anyone from re-checking it for seven releases.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **Rung 6 / hardware bring-up.** No DE10-Nano and no SuperStation One are
  attached — confirmed by checking the USB bus, serial devices, removable block
  devices and mounts. Unchanged since v2.6.6.
- **`ppuoamcorrupt052`'s asymmetry itself.** Ledger 3.19 says resolving it needs
  a source neither repository has. It is allowed and attributed, not resolved.
- **C1**, the `$2006` v-copy delay depth, still under-determined at 1-4 dots.
