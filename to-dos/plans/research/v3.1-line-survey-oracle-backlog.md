# RustyNES oracle backlog: every open, deferred or proposed item (input to the v3.1 → v4.0 roadmap)

Read-only survey of `release/v3.0.1` (head `1bf6f14f`, 2026-10-06). v3.0.0
"Cornerstone" is the current release; v3.0.1 "Mortar" is in progress. Every
status below was checked against the tree, the CHANGELOG or GitHub. A source
document's own status word was not trusted on its own. **UNVERIFIED** marks
anything this pass could not settle.

**Firewall note.** No reference-emulator source and no third-party NES HDL was
opened. One incidental contact is reported under PROV-02: a TODO grep across
`crates/` matched comment lines inside the vendored TriCNES C# tree. Those lines
are not reproduced here and were not used.

**Legend.**

- **Status:**
  - OPEN: real and not done.
  - PARTIAL: some of it shipped.
  - SHIPPED: done (where is cited).
  - OBSOLETE: the premise no longer holds.
  - BY-DESIGN: a deliberate non-target.
  - MANUAL: code is complete and only a human, device, account or hosted deploy can verify it.
- **Size:** S is under a day, M is days, L is a release or more.
- **Blocker:** nothing, hardware, device, upstream, maintainer decision, or external (dumps, hosting, accounts).
- **Breaks:** the effect on compatibility.
  - "none" means additive or off by default.
  - "behaviour" means emulation output changes. ADR 0045 then requires an `EMULATION_EPOCH` bump, which refuses older movies and netplay peers. That has been done in a patch (v3.0.1 raised the epoch 1 → 2), so it is not by itself a MAJOR trigger in current practice.
  - "state" means a save-state section version moves.
  - "movie" or "netplay" means a format or protocol bump.
  - "API" means a public Rust API break, which is a MAJOR trigger under `VERSION-PLAN.md`.

## (b) Items that can only land in a MAJOR (v4.0.0), or that would make one

Under `VERSION-PLAN.md` a MAJOR is a public-API break, a save-state-format break, or a new deliverable class (ADR 0041).

**What actually needs a MAJOR.**

1. **Adding a variant to any exhaustive public enum.**
   - T-API-EXTENSIBLE (v3.0.0) made four structs `#[non_exhaustive]`, but most public enums are still exhaustive. By a rough count of `pub enum` against an attribute-adjacent `#[non_exhaustive]`:
     - `rustynes-core`: about 7 of 20 are non-exhaustive
     - `rustynes-mappers`: 6 of 27
     - `rustynes-apu`: 2 of 7
     - `rustynes-ppu`: 1 of 6
     - `rustynes-cpu`: 1 of 2
     - `rustynes-netplay`: 1 of 14
   - Features above that would add a variant (FE-01 or FE-02 needing an option enum, CR-03 a new input-event kind, new error variants) are API breaks wherever the enum is exhaustive.
   - **Candidate v4.0.0 break:** mark the remaining public enums `#[non_exhaustive]` in one go, the enum half of T-API-EXTENSIBLE. This is the only item found that *must* be a MAJOR.
2. **MOB-04, the borrowed-framebuffer bridge API.** It breaks the `rustynes-mobile` UniFFI surface (generated Kotlin/Swift). The bridge is in-repo, so it is a MAJOR only if the bridge counts as public API, which is a maintainer call.
3. **Removing anything deprecated.** None today: `rg '#\[deprecated'` finds nothing outside `vendor/`.

**Format breaks a MAJOR could batch, though current practice lands them in minors and patches.**

- CR-03 (`.rnm` format plus input stream; possibly the netplay protocol).
- FE-01 and FE-02 (option carriage in `.rnm` and `config_digest`, which means a netplay protocol bump).
- ACC-03 (phi2 write placement: behaviour, state, goldens).
- ACC-04 (behaviour plus state).

Practice so far:

- v2.9.5 raised `PPU_SNAPSHOT_VERSION` in a minor.
- v2.9.9 raised `.rnm` to format 4 in a minor.
- v3.0.1 raises `EMULATION_EPOCH` in a patch.

The project therefore treats behaviour, state and movie breaks as non-MAJOR in practice, while `VERSION-PLAN.md` still lists "save-state-format breaks" as a MAJOR trigger. This needs reconciling; see decision (c)3.

**The new-deliverable-class trigger.** The first hardware-verified FPGA core (ADR 0041, kept available by ADR 0043 Decision 2) may itself be v4.0.0 rather than v3.x.
