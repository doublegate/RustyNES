# v3.4.1: the hosted services deployed, and v3.4.0's MiSTer leftovers

A patch release on the v3.4.0 "Waypoint" line, written on 2026-10-18 after
v3.4.0 shipped. It carries what v3.4.0 deliberately left for it, and nothing
that would change what v3.4.0 is.

## What v3.4.0 closed, and what it left

v3.4.0 shipped the hosted services (`services/hosted/`) as code with no build
pointing at them, because the maintainer was away with limited connectivity
(2026-10-17, [`v3.4.0-plan.md`](v3.4.0-plan.md)). It also shipped its two MiSTer
bitstreams past a `release-rbf.sh` refusal, by the maintainer's decision ("don't
rebuild them -- post as is"): a Quartus 10027 width warning at
`rtl/cart/cart.sv` line 1376 and the Zapper aim's constant ninth bit. Sibling
PR #64's second review round found two save-engine defects on a cartridge
change, deferred here by the maintainer.

## Decisions (maintainer, 2026-10-18)

- **Scope of the hosted base:** the Pages build, the desktop release binaries,
  and the Android Play / sideload and iOS TestFlight CI builds. F-Droid is
  excluded.
- **Web RetroAchievements is held** until the RetroAchievements integration
  request (D31) is sent and answered. The Pages build ships WITHOUT
  `browser-cheevos`; the browser packaging is fixed anyway, so the feature works
  when it is turned on. Following from that (the agent's call, recorded here):
  the deployed Worker has `RA_ENABLED` `"false"`, so nothing is forwarded to
  RetroAchievements under the `RustyNES/` User-Agent before RA agrees.
- **The RA proxy limits** stay as proposed: 240 / 10 / 50,000.
- **Release bitstream names:** the GitHub release assets are version-named
  (`RustyNES_MiSTer-<tag>.rbf`, `-lite.rbf`); the sibling's `releases/` keeps the
  datecoded names Distribution_MiSTer parses.

## Out of scope

- Enabling `browser-cheevos` on Pages, or the RA proxy, before D31 is answered.
- The 30-day TURN egress reading (README step 7): it is a v3.4.x follow-up by
  definition.
- Anything from v3.5.0, which opens with the Lite-tier decision (the on-die
  build is at 88% of the device's logic).

## Risks

- The hosted base is baked in at compile time, so a Worker URL change needs a
  rebuild of every client; a user's `hosted_base_url` overrides it.
- The bitstreams must be recompiled for rows 9-10, and a seed chosen for
  v3.4.0's RTL describes that RTL only.
