# v3.6.2: the latest AccuracyCoin, and Rad Racer's ground flicker

A patch release after v3.6.1 "Threshold" (2026-11-05), planned 2026-11-05 from
the maintainer's instruction: update to the most recent AccuracyCoin and pass
all of it, and fix the flicker "on the right-hand side of *Rad Racer* in the
ground texture when the car is driving", which earlier fixes had not removed.
The codename is asked at the cut.

## Results recorded during the release

- **Row 1.** Upstream `f5f41dc2..66e87112` is twelve commits: one new test,
  `DMC Playback Rate` (`$0498`), revisions to `DMA Sync Pretest` and `DMA
  Landing on Write`, a stack fix in `Misaligned OAM Behavior`, and extra checks
  in the DMC-DMA tests. 152 catalog rows, 147 scored. The new ROM passed 147 of
  147 on its first run, with no emulator change. The epoch fingerprint gained a
  `rom_fnv` column so the ROM swap is a new probe (blessed in two steps).
- **Row 2, the mechanism.** Line 139 of the race is the road's mirroring
  split: `$2006` = `$23`, `$00`, then the MMC1 mirroring bit. On about one
  frame in eleven the game's sprite-0 poll ends one loop early and the whole
  raster chain runs 20 dots sooner (`rad_racer_prov --events`), so the delayed
  `t -> v` copy lands between an attribute fetch's ALE and read halves. The
  live-`v` hybrid read `$23EE`: palette 0, an 8-pixel sky-coloured dash in
  the sand (`rad_racer_prov --rect`). 69 of 800 frames.
- **Row 2, the oracles.** Mesen (black box, its libretro core driven by
  `lrfront.py`, output only) shows 0 of 801 frames, but models no hybrid fetch.
  A 2012 nesdev post reports a right-side glitch on that line on a real NES.
  TriCNES could not be consulted (its harness only logs APU writes here, and
  its source is open to this project only for AccuracyCoin work). A
  `COPY_V_DELAY` of 3 left the dash (81 of 800), so the delay is not the lever.
- **Row 2, the decision (maintainer, 2026-11-05):** the hybrid stays on the
  nametable read (the case AccuracyCoin tests); the attribute read takes the
  address its ALE half drove. 0 of 800 and 0 of 1,200 (a steering run; Mesen
  0 of 1,200). Emulation epoch 10. ADR 0030 amendment. Not hardware-verified.

## Re-scope, 2026-11-06 (the maintainer)

The maintainer reported *Rad Racer*'s glitches still present on the v3.6.2 build, including where the ground meets the horizon. Row 2's gate was met on a metric that counted only sky-coloured pixels, and the fix behind it rested on a wrong diagnosis:

- the dash was still drawn on 68 to 73 of 800 race frames, now grey;
- it comes from the NAMETABLE read's hybrid (`$23FA`), not the attribute read;
- the copy lands four to six dots earlier on those frames, not twenty.

A 2012 nesdev report places a glitch on that line on a real NES. Asked how to proceed, the maintainer chose a per-game correction, on by default for *Rad Racer*, and the withdrawal of the attribute rule in both cores.
